# Website workflow

- Do not automatically push commits or deploy this website.
- For every website change, build and run a local preview, then share the preview URL and the changes with the user.
- Wait for explicit human approval of the preview before pushing to GitHub or deploying to Cloudflare. A request to edit the site alone is not approval to publish it.
- Keep changes scoped to the website repository. Preserve unrelated user changes.

# Blog release checks

- New blogs must strictly match the VALG and MLtheorybench article format. Before publishing, compare the built output with both: bilingual article rendering and language persistence, the shared light/dark control and theme persistence, top bar and return-to-Blog navigation, working internal and external hyperlinks, figures, citations, and a references section whose entries are cited in the body.
- Check both languages and both themes in a browser at desktop and phone widths. Test links from the homepage and Blog index into the article, and from the article back to the Blog index.
- On phones, Blog and About us must be visible as direct navigation links without opening a menu. Keep them usable in both languages and at narrow widths.
- Choose each Blog entry thumbnail from a figure or table in its paper or article. Pick one that communicates the central idea or most important finding at a glance, including a results figure when it does that well. Check it at card size in light and dark themes.
- Display Blog entries in reverse chronological order by their published date on both the homepage and Blog index. Keep author names readable at phone width.
- Keep thumbnails compact and unframed. Place titles, dates, authors, and links in the article listing instead of adding summary labels around the image.
